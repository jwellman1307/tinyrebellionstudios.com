The Studio

Built independently and with intention.

Every project starts with a recognizable problem, pressure, or experience rather than a list of features looking for a purpose.

Practical

Apps solve focused, understandable problems without unnecessary complexity.

Meaningful

Games are built around systems, consequences, and recognizable human experiences.

Respectful

Products should be clear about what they do, what they store, and what they ask from the user.

Privacy

Privacy built into the studio.

Tiny Rebellion Studios believes software should ask for as little as possible. Projects are designed to avoid unnecessary accounts, tracking, and personal-data collection whenever the experience can work without them.
