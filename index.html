Between Checks

A clearer view of the money you have right now.

Between Checks is built for people who need a useful answer to one immediate question: what can I safely spend before I get paid again? The app keeps the calculation understandable and stores budgeting information locally on the device.

Understand the number

See exactly where Safe to Spend comes from.

The spending breakdown separates available cash, bills due before payday, and an optional cash cushion so the final amount is easy to follow instead of hidden behind a complicated budget.

Plan before buying

Test a purchase before it changes your balance.

The What if I spend tool previews how a purchase would affect available cash, Safe to Spend, and the daily amount remaining before the next paycheck.

Core Screens

Income, bills, expenses, and history in one place.

The app stays focused on the current pay period while still giving users the records and controls needed to keep the numbers accurate.

Income

The Studio

Built independently and with intention.

Every project starts with a recognizable problem, pressure, or experience rather than a list of features looking for a purpose.

01

Practical

Apps are designed to solve focused, understandable problems without unnecessary complexity getting in the way.

02

Meaningful

Games are built around pressure, consequences, systems, and choices that connect to recognizable human experiences.

03

Respectful

Products should be clear about what they do, what they store, and what the person using them is being asked to provide.

Privacy

Privacy where it matters.

Tiny Rebellion Studios avoids unnecessary accounts, tracking, cloud storage, and personal-data collection whenever the product can work without them.

Between Checks stores budgeting information locally on the user's device and does not require access to a bank account. Financial tools should help people understand their money without demanding control of it.
