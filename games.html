Definitely Productive

A satirical office-life game about surviving the workweek, managing energy and stress, completing enough work to stay employed, and climbing a deeply questionable corporate ladder.

  • Choose how to spend each workday
  • Balance production, visibility, stress, and energy
  • Respond to office events and interruptions
  • Face end-of-day and weekly performance reviews
  • Manage housing, transportation, and life outside work
The Workday Loop

Start the day, make choices, and live with the result.

The core loop moves from planning the workday through action cards and into an end-of-day review.
